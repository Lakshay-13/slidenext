The Cosmic Scale

The universe runs on Differential Equations.

From galaxy clusters to the flow of air over a wing, everything follows laws that describe continuous change over space and time.

Astrophysics

Evolution of stars and the curvature of spacetime.

Weather

Chaotic atmospheric turbulence and climate modeling.

Digital Twins

Real-time physics for complex industrial systems.

The Bottleneck

Traditional solvers are too expensive.

Finite Element Methods and Computational Fluid Dynamics are hitting the limits of modern supercomputing.

Grid Dependency

Discretizing space into millions of cells is slow. High resolution simulations can take weeks on massive HPC clusters.

Scaling Failure

The Curse of Dimensionality makes high-dimensional problems impossible for standard grid-based solvers.

The Intuition

Solving physics via Approximation.

Instead of calculating discrete points, what if we learned the entire solution field as a function?

Neural networks are Universal Function Approximators. We can train them to represent physical solutions u(x,t) directly.

u(x, t) ≈ NN(x, t; θ)
The Secret Sauce

The Power of Autograd.

Because neural networks are mathematically differentiable, we can compute exact physics derivatives inside the network.

"Neural networks allow us to turn physics equations into trainable loss components."

Concept

Meet the PINN.

Physics-Informed Neural Networks embed physical laws (PDEs) directly into the training loop.

ℒ = λdℒdata + λpℒphysics

ℒdata

Fit the sparse measurements or sensors we have from the real world.

ℒphysics

Enforce the governing equations (PDE residuals) at all points.

The Research Gap

PINNs are unstable to train.

Manual balancing of loss weights (λ) is a black art—slow, fragile, and frustrating.

λ-Tuning Hell

Hyperparameters like learning rate and loss weights interact in chaotic ways. One wrong choice lead to total failure.

Slow Convergence

Satisfying physics constraints often plateaus for thousands of epochs before any meaningful progress is made.

Our Solution

Optimization as a Game.

Reinforcement Learning (RL) excels at discovering optimal strategies through trial and error.

The RL Controller

An agent observes the Loss Trajectory and takes actions to dynamically tune the PINN hyperparameters in real-time.
